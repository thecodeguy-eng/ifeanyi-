Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners. We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our platform, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our services.

Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary

Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the Platform to function. They enable core functionality such as security, login session management, and account authentication. These cannot be disabled.

Examples: Session cookies, CSRF tokens, authentication state

Functional

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your settings and preferences, such as your selected language, currency preferences, and chart configurations, so you don't need to re-configure them every visit.

Examples: Language selection, UI preferences, chart settings

Analytics

Performance Cookies

These cookies collect anonymized information about how visitors use the Platform — which pages are visited most, error messages encountered, and how users navigate. This helps us improve the platform.

Examples: Page view tracking, session duration, feature usage

Security

Security Cookies

These cookies help detect and prevent suspicious activity, protect against fraud, and maintain the security of your account by monitoring unusual access patterns.

Examples: Fraud detection, device fingerprinting, IP verification

Cookie Retention

Cookies may be either session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (retained for a set period). Our cookies are retained as follows:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when browser is closed
  • Authentication Cookies: 30 days or until logout
  • Preference Cookies: 12 months
  • Analytics Cookies: 90 days (anonymized)

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to notify you when cookies are being set. Note that disabling essential cookies may prevent certain features of the Platform from functioning correctly.

Instructions for managing cookies in common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services embedded in our Platform (such as TradingView charts) may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those services. We do not allow third-party advertising cookies on our Platform.

Contact
