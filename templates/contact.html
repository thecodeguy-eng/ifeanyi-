Frequently Asked

How long do deposits take?

Crypto deposits are credited after 1 network confirmation, typically 10–30 minutes depending on the blockchain.

How do I withdraw funds?

Go to Dashboard > Withdrawal. Enter your amount and wallet address. Processing takes 24–48 hours after manual review.

Is my money safe?

Yes. Funds are held in segregated accounts. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and store 95% of crypto in cold wallets.
