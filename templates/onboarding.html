🔒 Protect Your Account

Essential security tips to keep your funds safe

Use a Strong Password

Never share your password with anyone. Use a unique password that combines uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Add an extra layer of security to your account. You can enable this in Settings → Security.

Beware of Phishing

Always verify you're on the official Influxfinancetrading Global website. Never click suspicious links in emails or messages.

Verify Withdrawal Addresses

Always double-check wallet addresses before withdrawing. Transactions are irreversible.
